# BK First Bot (free)

A free setup guide bot by BK Han · AI Hustle Generation. It teaches email and calendar basics, records your license ID, and deploys **Map Lead Hunter**, the paid lead-finding bot for a brochure / landing-page side hustle.

## Quick start (5 steps)
1. Get your license ID (MLH-XXXX) from the class signup page. Send all emails from the email you signed up with.
2. Create a new Grok Bot and paste in [FIRST_BOT_INSTRUCTIONS.md](FIRST_BOT_INSTRUCTIONS.md) as its instructions.
3. Say "hi" and follow its lessons and setup questions.
4. Send the SETUP email to INBOX_ADDRESS ([email formats](EMAIL_FORMATS.md)). BK's reply contains the Map Lead Hunter instructions.
5. Create your Map Lead Hunter bot from that reply, then send LEADS emails in the same format.

Full guide: [STUDENT_SETUP_GUIDE.md](STUDENT_SETUP_GUIDE.md) · Adapting it for other audiences: [CUSTOMIZE.md](CUSTOMIZE.md)

All communication is by email with your license ID in the subject. Your bots only draft; you send.
