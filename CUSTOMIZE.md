# Customize this pattern for other audiences

The First Bot pattern is generic: **free guide bot → teaches basics → collects setup answers → deploys a paid specialist bot → everything reports back by email with a license ID in the subject.**

To adapt it:
1. **Audience:** replace "side-hustlers" with yours, e.g. [realtors], [tuition centres], [personal trainers].
2. **Niche and area questions:** change the examples, e.g. for realtors: target = [HDB upgraders in Punggol], [landlords with condos in District 15].
3. **Specialist bot:** swap Map Lead Hunter for the bot you sell, e.g. [Social Media Manager], [Newsletter Manager]. Keep the "draft only, human sends" rule.
4. **Offer:** replace "brochure or landing page" with what the end customer buys, e.g. [listing landing page], [monthly social posts].
5. **Email types:** keep `SETUP | <ID> | <Name>`. Rename `LEADS` to your result type if needed, e.g. `POSTS | <ID> | <week> | <count>`. Always keep the license ID in the subject.
6. **ID prefix:** use a different prefix per product, e.g. `RLT-XXXX` for realtors, so the backend knows which product it is.
7. **Compliance:** keep the PDPA / Do Not Call rules for Singapore, and add your industry's rules (for example, CEA advertising guidelines for realtors).
8. **Pricing block:** update the "Key facts" section with your own prices.

Keep [brackets] for anything the student fills in.
